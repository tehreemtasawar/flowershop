Our Story

Our shop started as a small stall that sold fresh roses to families in the neighbourhood.

Today we work with local growers every morning, so every bouquet is arranged the same day it is delivered. From birthdays to mehndi and wedding functions, we help make every occasion colourful.

Our Bouquets

Gulab Bouquet

A bouquet of fresh red and pink roses, commonly used for birthdays and family events.

Seasonal Mix

A mix of gerberas, lilies, and carnations, based on what is available locally in season.

Shaadi Special

An arrangement of gainda (marigold) and roses, used for mehndi and wedding functions.
